Nudge
A gentle push in the right direction. Master any complex desktop app without ever leaving your canvas.

What is Nudge?
Modern creative and technical software—from 3D suites like Blender to video editors, DAWs, and CAD packages—comes with steep learning curves and menus buried dozens of levels deep. When you hit a roadblock, the default routine is frustrating: pause your work, open a browser, search forums, and scrub through 15-minute video tutorials just to locate a single nested setting.

Nudge is an intelligent visual companion designed to eliminate that friction completely.

Hovering weightlessly alongside your mouse, Nudge watches your active workspace from the background. The moment you are stuck or unsure of the next step, press a quick shortcut and ask for what you need. Instead of reading blocks of text or watching someone else work, Nudge visually points the way right on your screen—illuminating menus, highlighting controls, and demonstrating the exact clicks needed to bring your idea to life.

Key Features
Visual In-App Guidance

No split screens, external browser tabs, or confusing text instructions. Nudge drops subtle visual markers and glowing rings directly over the buttons, sliders, and tools you need inside your active workspace.

Two Ways to Interact

Guide Mode (Learn by Doing): Nudge acts as a friendly tutor. It glides over to point out where to click next, explains the rationale, and steps aside so muscle memory stays with you.

Auto Mode (Hands-Off Execution): For repetitive tasks, tedious menu navigation, or quick setups, let Nudge handle the clicks, drags, and keystrokes directly while you sit back.

Universal Compatibility

Nudge doesn't require bespoke plugins, API integrations, or third-party add-ons. If an app runs on your desktop display, Nudge can interpret its layout and navigate its interface.

Natural Conversation

Ask questions the way you think:

"How do I unwrap UVs for this mesh?"

"Add a warm rim light behind this object."

"Where is the color management panel in this version?"
